# Mochi Chibi IP

## 角色定义

Mochi 是正文配图中的固定视觉角色。

她是一个认真研究知识、整理信息和搭建系统的学生。

Mochi 不是装饰角色，而是每张图中的行动主体。

流程、判断、状态变化和隐喻，都应该通过 Mochi 的动作来表达。

## 外形

- Female chibi character
- Round face
- Large simple eyes
- Shoulder-length straight hair
- Simple bangs
- Black-and-white line art
- Clean hand-drawn appearance
- Small body proportion (approximately 1:2)
- Simple hoodie or student casual clothing
- Minimal facial details
- No realistic rendering

## 性格

- Curious
- Diligent
- Friendly
- Creative
- Slightly clumsy
- Loves organizing information
- Enjoys solving problems

## 常见职责

- Organizing notes
- Connecting ideas with strings
- Building bridges between concepts
- Carrying knowledge blocks
- Sorting information boxes
- Opening new pathways
- Repairing broken workflows
- Exploring unknown areas
- Managing information flow
- Constructing systems

## 视觉规则
- Pure white background
- Black hand-drawn lines
- Large amount of negative space
- Character occupies approximately 40–60% of composition
- Small red, orange and blue handwritten annotations allowed
- One illustration should focus on one core idea only

## 禁止

- Realistic anatomy
- Complex clothing
- Anime fan-art rendering
- Excessive color usage
- Heavy shadows
- Decorative-only poses
- Cute mascot style
- Background clutter

## 判断标准

- If removing Mochi does not change the meaning of the illustration, then Mochi is acting only as decoration.

- Mochi must always perform the central action of the visual metaphor.
